Privacy notice

A scan is sent only after the person using the device enters a valid one-time code and explicitly accepts the scan and report submission.

Who receives the report

The report is linked to the owner account that created the code and is visible to that owner and the site administrator. The client cannot select a different recipient.

Report contents and retention

Reports may contain device identifiers, operating-system and hardware details, antivirus status, scan coverage and findings. Reports remain stored until the responsible owner or administrator deletes them.
