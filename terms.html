For owners and staff of servers using Blue Detected.

1. Purpose

Blue Detected provides a tool that allows server staff to request a PC anti-cheat check from a player, with their consent, and review the report.

2. Staff Responsibilities

  • Inform the player before the check and obtain their free and explicit consent; never force the tool on someone who has not agreed.
  • Use reports only for anti-cheat moderation on your server and do not publish them publicly.
  • Do not conclude that cheating occurred based on a single indicator: the report is an indicator that must be reviewed manually.
  • Comply with applicable data protection laws and delete reports that are no longer needed (automatic deletion after {{DAYS}} days).
