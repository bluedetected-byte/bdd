FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Important information before you begin.

How are results linked to a player?

Each check uses a unique code. The report submitted by the application is linked to that code and appears in the account that created it.

Does the score automatically mean the player is cheating?

No. Scores and detections are indicators. Staff should review the details before making a decision.

How do I access the dashboard?

Sign in with Discord, then activate a valid key if your account requests it.
